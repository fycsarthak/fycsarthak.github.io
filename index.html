01   ABOUT ME

I'm Sarthak, a 18-year-old ethical hacker and web developer driven by the challenge of breaking systems before the bad guys do.

My journey started with web development — building responsive interfaces with React and diving deep into how the web actually works. That curiosity pulled me straight into cybersecurity, where I now spend my time hunting bugs, testing web applications, and learning something new every single day.

Currently active on TryHackMe Top 20%, submitting reports on HackerOne and Bugcrowd, and working toward becoming a full-stack security professional. The goal is simple: understand every layer of the stack, then break it responsibly.
